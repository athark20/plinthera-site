Privacy notice

Last updated 2 November 2026

We keep only what you send us, we use it to reply to you, and we never sell it. This website sets no cookies and runs no trackers.

Who we are

Plinthera is an AI transformation and sports-technology consultancy based in Pune, India. For anything about your data, write to hello@plinthera.com or message +91 81778 33697 on WhatsApp.

What we collect

  • What you send us. The contact form does not send anything to us by itself. It puts your name, work email, company, company size, what you are looking for, where you operate, your main platform and your description of the work into a message, which you then choose to send us on WhatsApp or by email. We receive that message and your number or address.
  • Server logs. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like every web host, GitHub records visitors' IP addresses to keep the service secure. We do not receive these logs.

Please do not send payment details, identity documents or health information through this website; we do not need them.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or social-media trackers. Fonts and images are served with the site itself, so opening it does not send your details to anyone else.

Why we use it

To reply to your enquiry, arrange a call or a venue walkthrough, prepare a proposal you asked for and, if you become a client, to run that engagement. We do not add you to a newsletter or mailing list, and we do not make automated decisions about you.

If you are in the UK or the EU, we rely on your request (steps before a possible contract) and our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries. If you are in India, you consent to this use when you send the form, and you can withdraw that consent at any time by writing to us.

Who can see it

Plinthera's own team. WhatsApp and our email provider carry your messages to us under their own terms. We share your details with anyone else only where the law requires us to. We never sell personal data.

Where it is stored

In our WhatsApp and business email accounts. Their servers may be in a different country from yours; where they are, we rely on those providers' contractual safeguards for international transfers.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for up to 24 months after our last conversation and then deleted. If you become a client, we keep records for the length of the engagement and afterwards for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop using it. Write to hello@plinthera.com; we reply within 30 days, usually much sooner. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live: in India, the Data Protection Board of India; in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office; in the UAE, the UAE Data Office.

Children

This website is for businesses. It is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change how we use personal data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
